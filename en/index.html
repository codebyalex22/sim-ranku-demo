Why us

Three rules we stick to every day

  1. Dough rests for 24 hours

    A long ferment with a live sourdough starter makes bread more flavorful and easier to digest.

  2. Only natural ingredients

    82% butter, flour from family-run mills and seasonal berries. Ask at the counter for full ingredients.

  3. Ordering made simple

    Send a request — we'll confirm the details by messenger and have it ready when you need it.
